      Recent repos

      The popup lists repos you have opened on github.com recently. That list is worked out from the address of tabs you open, is stored only on this computer, and is never sent anywhere. Pause it any time with the Pause link at the bottom of the popup.

      Recent history cleared.

      Usage stats

      Counts which parts of the extension get used, so it can be improved. Never your organization names, repo names, or anything you visit. Off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off again whenever you like.

      What gets sent when it's on

      • That the popup was opened
      • That an organization was opened, and from which button
      • That a repo was opened, and whether from favorites or recents
      • That a repo was starred or unstarred
      • That tracking was paused or resumed
      • Roughly how many organizations and favorites you have, in bands

      Never a name, a URL, or anything identifying you.
